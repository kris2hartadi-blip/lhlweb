      <section id="who-we-are" class="scroll-mt-36 bg-white p-8 sm:p-10 rounded-2xl border border-slate-200 shadow-sm">
        <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-900 mb-6 pb-3 border-b border-slate-100 flex items-center gap-3">
          <span class="w-2.5 h-6 bg-blue-600 rounded-full"></span>
          Who We Are & What We Do
        </h2>
        <div class="space-y-4 text-slate-600 leading-relaxed text-sm sm:text-base">
          <p>Welcome to the LHL site. This is a safe space for discussion and collaboration, real time exchanges, and gathering of experiences about local humanitarian leadership (LHL)[cite: 1].</p>
          <p>The website is run by a Community of Practice of local and national actors with support from allies in the international NGOs whose aim is to ensure that LHL approaches are considered & embedded in any humanitarian preparedness, response and changing approaches[cite: 1].</p>
          <p>The group represents different thematical expertise from diverse geographical contexts all over the world[cite: 1]. The LHL aims to realize a transformation in the humanitarian sector that is locally led in line with the localization commitments of the 2016 World Humanitarian Summit which also led to two landmark documents, the Grand Bargain and the Charter for Change (C4C)[cite: 1].</p>
          
          <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-slate-900 pt-4 mb-2">What We Do</h3>
          <p>The group aims to ensure that local humanitarian leadership (LHL) approaches are considered & embedded in any humanitarian preparedness, response, recovery and changing approaches[cite: 1]. The scope can be country, regional and/or global depending on emerging needs[cite: 1]. Core of the practice group is programmatic; however, it should inform/support the Influencing work where/when required[cite: 1]. Since most of the local organizations in the LHL network work beyond response, we also aim to work in the triple Nexus goals of development, peacebuilding, and response[cite: 1].</p>
          <p>Among the gaps seen by the group is low representation of Women’s Rights Organization (WRO) in its ranks[cite: 1]. Effective humanitarian response requires promoting and strengthening women’s leadership in local humanitarian action[cite: 1]. Supporting women and women’s organizations to play key leadership roles in the humanitarian sector and in community DRR, relief, and recovery efforts—helping them highlight the confidence and voice that is so often muted—is an overarching objective of the practice group too[cite: 1]. This will help reduce gender inequality, increase inclusion/diversity, and improve the effectiveness of humanitarian action[cite: 1]. Hence, we continue to look for WROs to join the practice group[cite: 1].</p>
        </div>
      </section>

          <li>Support progress on LHL commitments including those in the Charter for Change and the Grand Bargain[cite: 1].</li>
          <li>Enable the mainstreaming of LHL principles and approaches in humanitarian preparedness, response initiatives & ways of working; strive for quality & relevance of humanitarian approach through the LHL lens[cite: 1].</li>
          <li>Gather & exchange emerging knowledge and learning that is relevant to the LHL agenda in a changing humanitarian context/model[cite: 1].</li>
          <li>Scope future scenarios & possibilities on how issues impact the humanitarian sector, particularly the localization/LHL agenda and potentially be an innovative force of proposition for new ideas/approaches etc[cite: 1].</li>

          <p>As a community of practice, the practice group is being run by a core group of Oxfam staff and local and national NGOs and local humanitarian agencies on a voluntary and rotational basis[cite: 1]. Since the group is still new, the group continue to have a core group that stays informal in nature, but pays attention to the issues raised by the group during the monthly meetings and during email discussions[cite: 1]. The larger practice group takes a ‘whole of society approach’, where all the members are responsible for keeping the group running and making the engagements relevant[cite: 1].</p>

  <!-- SCRIPT: MOBILE MENU & SCROLLSPY HIGHLIGHT -->
  <script>
    document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', () => {
      // Mobile Menu Toggle
      const mobileMenuButton = document.getElementById('mobile-menu-button');
      const mobileMenu = document.getElementById('mobile-menu');

      mobileMenuButton.addEventListener('click', () => {
        mobileMenu.classList.toggle('hidden');
      });

      // ScrollSpy & Capsule Active State Highlight
      const sections = document.querySelectorAll('section[id]');
      const capsules = document.querySelectorAll('.nav-capsule');

      function changeActiveCapsule() {
        let scrollY = window.pageYOffset;

        sections.forEach(current => {
          const sectionHeight = current.offsetHeight;
          const sectionTop = current.offsetTop - 150;
          const sectionId = current.getAttribute('id');

          if (scrollY > sectionTop && scrollY <= sectionTop + sectionHeight) {
            capsules.forEach(capsule => {
              capsule.classList.remove('bg-blue-600', 'text-white', 'shadow-md');
              capsule.classList.add('bg-slate-100', 'text-slate-600');
              if (capsule.getAttribute('data-target') === sectionId) {
                capsule.classList.remove('bg-slate-100', 'text-slate-600');
                capsule.classList.add('bg-blue-600', 'text-white', 'shadow-md');
              }
            });
          }
        });
      }

      window.addEventListener('scroll', changeActiveCapsule);
      // Trigger once on load
      changeActiveCapsule();
    });
  </script>
